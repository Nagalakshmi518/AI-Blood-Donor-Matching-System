ADVANCED CAPABILITIES

Engineered for Rapid Critical Care

Our intelligent platform removes emergency delays through real-time algorithmic coordination.

Multi-Factor AI Ranking

Donors are ranked in real time using a multi-criteria model evaluating blood compatibility, geodesic distance, historical response probability, and reliability scores.

Geospatial GPS Dispatch

Calculates precise route distances between requesting hospitals and donor locations with Leaflet GIS mapping to minimize transit time during emergencies.

Automated Inventory Fallback

If no local donors are available or response deadlines expire, the system automatically redirects and fulfills requests from verified hospital blood bank reserves.

SYSTEM WORKFLOW

From Request to Transfusion in 4 Steps

End-to-end transparent coordination connecting patients, donors, and hospitals.

01

Request Creation

Patient or hospital submits an emergency request with required units, emergency priority, and hospital pin.

02

AI Optimization

The ranking algorithm scores all compatible nearby donors and dispatches prioritized notifications.

03

Donor Response

Ranked donors receive instant alerts with a 10-minute response countdown timer and route guidance.

04

Fulfilled Donation

Upon acceptance or hospital inventory fallback, the transfusion is safely coordinated and recorded.
