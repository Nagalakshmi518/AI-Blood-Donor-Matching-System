SMART TECHNOLOGY

Intelligent Emergency Response System

BloodNeed combines AI-powered donor matching, location intelligence, emergency prioritization, and hospital inventory support to help patients receive blood faster.

🤖

AI Donor Ranking

Donors are intelligently ranked based on blood compatibility, availability, reliability score, donation history, and response probability.

📍

Location-Based Matching

The system calculates the distance between donors and hospitals to identify nearby eligible donors during emergencies.

⚡

Emergency Priority

Critical and high-priority blood requests receive faster response handling with emergency-based donor response time windows.

🏥

Hospital Inventory Fallback

When eligible donors are unavailable, the system automatically checks hospital blood inventory to help fulfill the request.
